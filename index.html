SERVER RULES

To maintain a fun and fair environment for everyone, please adhere to these core rules.

X-Ray, fly hacks, auto-clickers above 15 CPS, and dupe exploits are strictly prohibited. Violators will face immediate ban without warnings.
Hate speech, toxicity, harassment, and spamming in global chat are not tolerated. Keep competition in-game and friendly.
Unclaimed builds are vulnerable in the wilderness, but using glitch mechanics to bypass land claims is bannable.
Scamming real-world currency or out-of-game assets is strictly forbidden. In-game trade agreements must be honored.
